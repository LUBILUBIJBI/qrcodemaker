QR Code Generator

How to create a QR code

Creating a QR code is simple. Follow the steps below.

1. Enter your text or link

Enter the text or link you want to turn into a QR code in G40.

2. Do not change G39

IMPORTANT: Do not change the link in G39.
This link is required for the QR code to work correctly.

3. Get your QR code

After entering your text or link into G40, the QR code will automatically be generated in A1.

⸻

Quick Overview

Cell	Action
G40	Enter your text or link
G39	Do not change
A1	QR code is generated here

⸻

That’s it!

Enter your text or link in G40, leave G39 unchanged, and the QR code will automatically appear in A1.
